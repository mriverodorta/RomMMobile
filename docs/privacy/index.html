RomMMobileS Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

RomMMobileS is an Android app maintained by HKloud. It connects to the RomM server you select to browse your library, download files, and optionally sync emulator saves.

Your RomM account and server

The app sends sign-in credentials or tokens to your chosen RomM server. It can also send Cloudflare Access credentials when you enable that connection option. The app encrypts saved credentials and tokens on your device using Android Keystore.

If you request a pairing code, RomMMobileS sends your device model and identifier to your RomM server to create the pairing request.

The app sends search terms to your RomM server when you search its library. It downloads library information and the files you request. Cover images may load from image hosts referenced by that library. Those hosts receive the connection information needed to serve the image.

The Google Play build uses HTTPS to encrypt connections in transit. The GitHub build also supports HTTP connections, including on local networks. HTTP does not encrypt the data sent over that connection.

Files and save sync

When you grant folder access, the app reads the selected folders to identify ROMs and emulator settings, and writes downloads or saves there. In the GitHub build, you can enable optional All Files Access. The app uses that access to find files, write to your chosen destinations, and watch save folders.

When save sync is enabled, the app sends the selected save types and related information to your RomM server. This can include filenames, file hashes, timestamps, and game identifiers. RomM also receives information to identify the syncing device, such as its model and a device identifier. The server stores the save files and controls the retention of its copies.

With Usage Access enabled, the app checks RetroArch activity and timestamps on your device to decide when it can safely restore a save. It does not upload this Android usage history.

Diagnostics and the website

The app keeps a rotating diagnostic log on your device. It can include errors, server information, and file paths. If you export or share a log, review its contents and choose who receives it. The app does not automatically send those logs to HKloud.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log connection information such as an IP address for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you request a Google Play tester invite through the form, HKloud stores your email address and submission time on its own server. If you request an invite by email, HKloud receives your email address and any details you send. HKloud uses this information to coordinate test invitations and feedback. If you are invited, HKloud may add your email address to the closed test in Play Console. You can request deletion using the contact below.

Retention and deletion

Signing out clears local sign-in credentials. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes its private settings, cache, logs, and recovery copies. Downloads and saves in shared folders remain there until you delete them. Exported files remain where you saved or shared them.

Manage server-side data through RomM or contact the operator of your RomM server. You can stop background save sync and revoke Android folder or Usage Access permissions at any time.

Contact

Send questions about this policy to HKloud at mriverodorta@gmail.com. Keep passwords and access tokens out of your message.
